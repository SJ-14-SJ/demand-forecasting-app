The `daily_demand.csv` file is an aggregate adaptation of Chen, D. (2015), Online Retail, UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Changes: UK-only filter; top three products by distinct positive-sale invoices in the first 120 calendar days; last partial day removed; cancellation rows excluded; sales aggregated and calendar gaps filled with zero. The original source is not endorsed by this project. See `provenance.json` for the checksum and selection details.

`synthetic_demand.csv` is generated demonstration data from the companion project. It is separate from UCI-derived measurements.
